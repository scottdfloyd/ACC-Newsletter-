We're Back — And We're at the Center of Everything

Hello, ACC family. After a brief hiatus, the Global Newsletter is BACK — and what a return it is. Because over the past quarter, the ACC community didn't just show up to culture. We created it. From Super Bowl LX to Coachella. From PaleyFest to The Devil Wears Prada 2. Wherever the conversation was happening, our community of 650+ marketing professionals was in the room — and most of the time, we were the ones running it.

Cross-agency collaborations are at an all-time high. New business is flowing. Our executive voices are echoing across every major industry stage. Be PROUD of this work. Spread the word. Let's go.

Coachella Was Our Stage

When it comes to Coachella 2026, the ACC family didn't just attend — we BUILT it. MKG and Pink Sparrow constructed Ed Sheeran's Old Phone Pub from the ground up in Ipswich, MA, then shipped it to the desert where Ed played a surprise set alongside Shaboozey and Alex Warren. The crowd lost their minds. We lost ours too — in the best possible way.

Meanwhile, MKG delivered Sol de Janeiro's Casa Cheirosa — the first fragrance activation in Coachella's history — and walked away with the Gold Award for Best Festival Activation from Event Marketer. HangarFour took the full ARRIVE Hotel for JeuveauFest: 150+ creators, 2M+ impressions, content running 6x above benchmark. That's not just a win. That's the ACC standard.

Super Bowl LX: We Were the Noise

At Super Bowl LX in San Francisco, the ACC community showed up everywhere. ACC brought Martha Stewart and seven local restaurants to Yerba Buena Gardens for Pepsi's Bay Area Local Eats — exceeding 2025 results. MKG transformed SF Brewing Co. into an immersive BÉIS takeover. ACC + DKC drove mass awareness for bubly's Nintendo/Illumination collab, landing USA Today and Access Daily coverage. This is what full-service looks like.

Where Culture Happens, We Show Up Cross-Agency Collaboration Creating Culture, Not Just Capturing It

Hello, ACC family. After a brief hiatus, the Global Newsletter is BACK — and what a return it is. Because over the past quarter, the ACC community didn't just show up to culture. We created it. From Super Bowl LX to Coachella. From PaleyFest to The Devil Wears Prada 2. From the Grammys stage to the boardrooms of POSSIBLE. Cross-agency collaborations are at an all-time high. New business is flowing. Our executive voices are echoing across every major industry stage. PMK Entertainment is hitting the ground running. Be PROUD of this work. Spread the word. Let's go.

Coachella Was Our Stage

When it comes to Coachella 2026, the ACC family didn't just attend — we BUILT it. MKG and Pink Sparrow constructed the Ed Sheeran x Atlantic Records Old Phone Pub from scratch in Ipswich, MA, then shipped it to the desert, where Ed played a surprise set alongside Shaboozey and Alex Warren. MKG's Sol de Janeiro Casa Cheirosa was the first-ever fragrance activation in Coachella history — and took Gold at Event Marketer.

Super Bowl LX: We Were the Noise

At Super Bowl LX in the Bay Area, ACC showed up everywhere. Martha Stewart, seven local restaurants, Yerba Buena Gardens — Pepsi's Local Eats exceeded 2025 results. MKG turned SF Brewing Co. into an immersive BÉIS takeover. ACC + DKC drove awareness for bubly x Nintendo's galaxy-inspired collab. USA Today, Access Daily, and more. This is what full-service looks like.

Entertainment Marketing: The ACC Standard

HangarFour's brand integration portfolio this quarter was stunning: Grey Goose fronted by Heidi Klum in The Devil Wears Prada 2. Dos Equis with Jon Hamm in Your Friends & Neighbors. Bohemia with Paul Rudd and Jack Black in Anaconda. PMK Entertainment wrapped PaleyFest LA at the Dolby Theatre with a Michael J. Fox surprise appearance. Press? TODAY Show, USA Today, People, Variety. That's the ACC standard.

Industry Voices: ACC at POSSIBLE

At the POSSIBLE conference, ACC's voice was in every major room. Michael Nyman sat down for NYSE-TV's Speaker's Corner interview and led the "Community is the New Channel" panel — featured in The Drum by EIC Gordon Young. Scott Floyd joined The Ad Podcast and the "Big Game" panel. Our presence at ADWEEK House, Female Quotient, and Unplugged Collective Supper Club made clear: ACC leads the industry conversation.
